Three documented relationships: `youth_headline_gap` (youth - headline, pp - the RQ1 divergence), `youth_ratio` (unitless multiple, None on zero headline), `neet_minus_unemployed` (the withdrawn/discouraged component NEET exposes but unemployment does not).
